[SECTION 1: PLAYER REGISTRATIONS]
All player registrations for the 2026 season must be processed through the official Palloliitto OmaFutis portal. The registration deadline is April 30, 2026. Salim Laanaya handles match logistics and registry administration.

The team's primary uniform color is dark blue. Players are responsible for maintaining their own gear. Replacement shin guards can be requested from the equipment manager.
